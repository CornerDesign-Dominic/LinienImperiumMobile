# Customer precious-goods artwork

The five special-contract categories use frameless transparent illustrations of their customers' existing precious goods instead of cropped region landscapes. `Projects` passes `customer.preciousCargo` from the authoritative project catalog to `ProjectCategoryArt`; the asset lookup is by commodity name, so adding or reordering customers cannot accidentally select another region's artwork. Unknown future goods use a neutral freight illustration.

Gold jewelry, fine tapestries, precious essences, jade artworks and amber carvings are category identity artwork only. The three normal customer products, referral cargo, payouts and contract rules remain unchanged. The referral category retains its entrusted coffer and its separately corrected fit.

All images use contain sizing with explicit width and height inside the bounded artwork column. Goods reserve space for a small region caption on the card paper; the former opaque landscape-label strip is removed. Each generated PNG is 384 px with true alpha. Exact built-in ImageGen prompts are bundled beside the assets.

Asset appearance and alpha were inspected. Native device layout verification is not available in this environment.
